Who am I?

My name is Denton, and I'm a full-time college student in school for cybersecurity. I've always had an interest in programming and high school was a great chance for me to learn more about computer science. Before the end of school I knew I wanted to continue my education but I wasn’t sure in what, my teacher mentioned cybersecurity one day, and that’s what I ultimately decided on in the end. Since starting college, I can confidently say I’m really happy with my decision. I’ve learnt a lot, gained so many new skills, met some really amazing people, and everyday I get to practice and work on some of my favourite things.

Why the blog?

The reason for this blog is to have somewhere for me to write and share what I’m doing. I want to share my journey while I work through school and anything I get to experience that is tech or cybersecurity related: things I’m learning, projects I’m working on, events I attend, and resources I use to practice. Even once I finish school, I want to continue to post here while searching for a job and while I’m in the industry. There is still a lot for me to learn, and writing it out gives me an even better opportunity of doing that.
