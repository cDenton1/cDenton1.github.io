Who am I?

My name is Denton, and I'm a college student in school for cybersecurity. I've had an interest in programming for a long time, which is ultimately what pushed me towards this field. Since starting college, I can confidently say I’m really happy with my decision, because everyday I get to work on something I'm passionate about.

Why the blog?

My reason for this blog is to have somewhere for me to write and share what I’m doing. I want to share what I'm learning, experiencing, and working on while I'm in school; whether cybersecurity related or not. There is still a lot for me to learn, and writing it out gives me the opportunity to reflect back on all of it.

Anti-AI Note

This may seem unecessary to include and may not be a popular opinion with some, but I stand behind this for moral and ethical reasons and I want people who view my site to be aware of this. Everything posted on this site is written by me, by myself, and it will continue to be like that as long as the site is alive. I avoid using AI whatsoever, having changed a lot of my apps and sites to alternatives that have less or no AI, and will eventually change away from GitHub as well.
