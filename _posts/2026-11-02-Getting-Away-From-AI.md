# Getting Away From AI

This isn't from like a "scared of AI" perspective but more so that I'm tired of everything having it and would rather not support companies who prefer it over real humans.

Everything nowadays is integrating it, and sometimes even forcing users to partake in training it by having them by default opt-in to their data being used to train models (i.e. Google taking users data from Drive, Gmail, etc.), even if the users don't use the AI.

And on top of it, some companies (like Google and Meta specifically) don't allow users to opt-out depending where they live in the world.

So here is everything I'm doing to get even further away from AI and begin removing large tech from my life.
