# I am Anti-AI

Not my usual type of post but this is something I have been thinking about recently and want to talk about, especially as a student studying cybersecurity: **I hate AI**.

I didn't know how to title this post, or frankly go about writing it. I have so many points and ideas about the topic and my opinion on it has massively changed ever since I first started learning about it but recently I fell into a spiral of really hating it.

I want to share my thoughts, especially as of recently.

## Where It Started

No, not AI history, instead my experience with AI. In high school, I knew very little about it and honestly had no interest in learning more about it; it sounded unnecessary at the time especially because I like learning and building things myself.

In college, a handful of my first year classes brought it up but mainly to say "hey, don't use this"; but as that year and the following two went on though, that changed. 

Classes this past month haven't exactly been pushing for it nor is it being super encouraged by instructors, but they are starting to allow the use of it for assignments. Along with that, there is more and more stuff popping up around campus about it, like workshops teaching people how to use it for example.

Sure, it can be a useful tool but I think how quickly it was developed especially over the last three years and how much companies are trying to push it onto consumers unconsensually is frustrating and is creating a dependency for a lot of people.

Back in about mid-September when a researcher had come out saying he couldn't work at these large AI companies anymore due to what is currently being developed (whether what he said was true or not) is when something finally snapped in me.

I had already been avoiding AI chatbots a lot this past year due to the environmental impact AI data centers have on the planet, but this was the point where I wanted to actively make a change beyond that: switching browsers, getting rid of or minimizing social media use, and so much more.

Some of those may sound unrelated but these last few weeks is where I really started to notice just how many companies were adding AI onto their platforms or right into their devices.

## Impact

A lot of people like to argue how useful AI can be and I won't argue against all those ideas, but I think most people are overusing it in areas that is completely unnecessary, in-turn developing dependencies on it.

I constantly sit around people who will use it for the most mundane, simple tasks instead of taking the time to think for themselves, ask the people around them, researching a little bit, or even just reading whatever they were already looking at.

And before someone argues with me that they are too busy or AI makes it faster, my arguments to that are: 

1) the more you do something, the faster you will get at it anyways but the more you use AI the slower you will be in the future without it or in some cases probably just losing the ability to do it at all - again, growing a dependency 
2) a lot of that falls onto capitalism and how current society is basically running on exploiting eachother, a whole separate topic but something that people are either ignoring or are completely unaware of

## The Change

A lot of these changes were already in the works, I have always been a conscious person when it comes to my environmental impact and am always trying to minimize how much I am wasting but AI kicked a lot of this into gear quite fast.
